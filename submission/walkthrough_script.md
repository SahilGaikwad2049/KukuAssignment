**0:20–0:45 — Episode Explorer**  
Select an episode and scan its timestamped screenplay timeline. Open dialogue, action, and camera events to show original event IDs and metadata.

**0:45–1:20 — Candidate Review**  
Filter to heuristic candidates. Open a candidate preview and show its complete chronological source events, hook, potential ending, timestamps, and family. Explain that a potential ending is not automatically a confirmed cliffhanger. Show the saved blind-viewer assessment and its evidence, then note that the pilot has eight validated assessments and two evidence-reference failures.

**1:20–1:45 — Rankings**  
Show continuous, composite, and combined leaderboards. Explain the six dimension weights and 0–100 provisional score. Show the pending human-decision status and that failed or unevaluated candidates do not receive an eligible score. The real composite leaderboard is honestly empty.

**1:45–2:15 — Semantic and composite capability**  
Describe how semantic analysis receives original parsed screenplay events and returns event IDs; Python constructs timestamp-valid candidates. Show the Series Workspace and Composite Promos empty state. If demonstrating the automated fixture, label it clearly as a synthetic test fixture, not a Kuku story discovery.

**2:15–2:45 — Validation and exports**  
Show the structural validator and the Exports page. Download final rankings or pilot reports. Explain that structural checks validate source and timing, while clarity, narrative impact, and human approval remain separate judgments.

**2:45–3:00 — Close**  
Summarize: heuristic candidates and a small blind-viewer pilot are demonstrated on saved outputs; semantic discovery, full-series analysis, and real composite promos remain to be run and reviewed.
